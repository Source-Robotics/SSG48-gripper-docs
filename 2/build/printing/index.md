# Printing the parts

## Material and settings

PETG for the body, the lids, the jaw plates and the coupler connector; the fin-ray fingers in a flexible filament.
The assembly manual's reference prints were made on a Prusa MK2S in PETG with 25 % infill, supports only where a
part needs them: clean every support off before assembly, the bearing seats and the rail slot in particular.

![The body printed in black and in grey, the two colours the shop sells](https://docs.source-robotics.com/SSG48-gripper-docs/2/assets/SSG48_black_and_grey.webp){ width="600" }

## The files

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The body, the lid, the gear and the magnet shaft, in two hardware versions: print the `V2` set, the large-bearing version ([Hardware versions](https://docs.source-robotics.com/SSG48-gripper-docs/2/01-overview/hardware-versions/)) | [Print files](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/SSG-48-adaptive-electric-gripper/tree/main/Print%20files) |
| The jaws and the guide cover (in the `V2` set), and the coupler connectors for PAROL6 and PAR6 (in `Couplers`) | the same folder |
| The fin-ray fingers and their jaw plate | [Print files/Jaws](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/SSG-48-adaptive-electric-gripper/tree/main/Print%20files/Jaws) |
| The STEP files, for a coupler connector of your own (the generic template) or your own fingers on the jaw plate | [STEP files](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/SSG-48-adaptive-electric-gripper/tree/main/STEP%20files) |

The brass inserts go in with a soldering iron after printing, as the assembly manual shows for each part.
