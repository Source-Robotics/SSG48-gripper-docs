body, the connector and its wires, the driver and the thermistor, the calibration, the linear guide, the lid, the
coupler connector, the cable. Read it once before you start: some steps cannot be undone without taking the body
apart again.

## Calibrate on the way

The motor is calibrated during the assembly, not before it: once the motor and the driver are in the body, the
wires soldered and the thermistor in the windings, and before the linear guide with the jaws goes on (page 22
of the manual), connect the serial adapter and run [Driver setup and first run](https://docs.source-robotics.com/SSG48-gripper-docs/1.1/build/first-run/). The racks would
block the calibration's turns, so the order matters.

## The cable

The gripper's cable is not in the box. [The cable](https://docs.source-robotics.com/SSG48-gripper-docs/1.1/04-installation/cable/) has the parts, the wiring
diagrams for PAROL6 and PAR6, and the warning that the two are not interchangeable; the repository's
[cable guide](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/SSG-48-adaptive-electric-gripper/blob/main/Assembly%20manual/Building_cables.md)
is the original.
